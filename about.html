the matilda effect

why a woman's name, hidden.

the Matilda effect describes what happens when a woman does the work and someone else gets the citation. it's named after Matilda Joslyn Gage, a 19th-century activist who wrote about exactly this — and was then largely written out herself.

money works the same way. the account in his name. the credit built on his file. the assets she can't find the paperwork for. we put her back in the name, and we hand over the information.
