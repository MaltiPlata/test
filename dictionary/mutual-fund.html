the dictionary · invest

mutual fund

one basket holding many investments. you buy a share of the basket instead of buying each thing.

20-sec readplain languageinvest

like you're 5

everyone chips in for one giant pizza with every topping. you own a slice of the whole pizza, not one sad topping.

use it in a sentence

“my 401(k) is just three mutual funds and i haven’t touched it in two years.”

good to know

the thing that matters most is the expense ratio — the annual fee, taken automatically. under 0.20% is normal for an index fund; over 1% is expensive and compounds against you for decades. mutual funds trade once a day after the market closes, which is a feature, not a flaw.
