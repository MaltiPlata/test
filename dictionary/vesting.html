vesting

your job promised you stock or 401(k) match — vesting is the waiting period before it’s actually yours.

20-sec readplain languagework

like you're 5

your boss says "stay 4 years and this treasure chest is yours." every year you stay, another chunk unlocks. leave early, and the locked part stays behind.

use it in a sentence

“i’m 75% vested, so if i quit today i’d walk away with three-quarters of my match.”

good to know

check your vest date before you accept a new offer — timing can be worth thousands.
